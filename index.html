Questions and answers

How do I start a game again?

Tap the back arrow at the top left to return Home, then tap New Game and choose a size and a level. This replaces the game you were playing; the New Game screen reminds you of that before you start.

If you choose a mistake limit in Settings, the sheet that appears when you reach it also offers “Try this puzzle again”, which restarts the same puzzle from the beginning.

Sound is not playing

Sound is off until you turn it on: open Settings from Home and switch on Sound. It plays only while a game is on screen. If you still hear nothing, check your device's silent setting and its volume. The app follows your device's silent setting, and your own music keeps playing alongside it.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device: the game you are in the middle of, your statistics and your settings. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere, so progress does not move between devices. If you delete the app, its data on your device is deleted with it.

How do I see the introduction again?

Open Settings from Home, find the How to play section and tap “Show the intro again”. Nothing else is reset. The same section has a short guide for each kind of puzzle.
