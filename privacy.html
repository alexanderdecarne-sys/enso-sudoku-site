Privacy policy

Enso Sudoku: Calm Puzzles, version 1.0.0
Last updated: 20 November 2026

Enso Sudoku collects no data.

  • Fully offline. The app has no networking code and never connects to the internet. It works the same with no connection at all.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. There is nothing to sign in to, nothing that shows ads, and nothing that measures what you do or follows you between apps or websites.
  • Your data stays on your device. Your game in progress, your statistics (such as times and how many puzzles you have finished) and your settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device, and the app never sends them anywhere. If you back up your device, iOS may include the app's data in your own backup; that backup is yours and is not something the app sends.
  • No third-party code. The app is built only with the frameworks Apple provides with iOS. It contains no code, SDKs or services from other companies.
  • No permissions. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or notifications.
  • Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else, so there is nothing for me to share, sell or delete on a server.

Two things worth knowing

  • Apple. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's Privacy settings, Apple may give me anonymous, combined crash and usage figures for the App Store. That is Apple's service, not part of the app, and it does not tell me who you are.
  • Deleting the app. Deleting the app deletes its data from your device. Copies in earlier backups of your device, if you made any, stay until you delete those backups.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above will change, and the app's update notes will say so.
